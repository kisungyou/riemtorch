Files: riemann-cities.rds, riemann-hands.rds, riemann-ERP.rds.
The complete R objects were loaded from cities.rda, hands.rda, and ERP.rda
and saved as version-2, xz-compressed RDS files. Values, ordering, labels,
and attributes are unchanged. Preprocessing is performed visibly in the
articles. riemann-snapshots.csv records source-file MD5 and snapshot SHA-256
checksums. extract-riemann.R reproduces the extraction from a local checkout.
These website-only files are excluded from the installed riemtorch package.

cities
------
60 cities in the contiguous United States with populations above 300,000
in the historical January-2006 description; city names, latitude/longitude,
unit Cartesian coordinates, and population counts. Riemann derived the
observations from maps::us.cities. These are historical data, not a current
population inventory. Source documentation:
https://github.com/kisungyou/Riemann/blob/e847692576f8b250c8140e1343b145f72caa379f/R/data_cities.R
https://cran.r-project.org/package=maps

hands
-----
40 left-hand configurations from four people, with 56 corresponding planar
landmarks per configuration. The first six configurations per person form a
sequence from maximally to minimally spread fingers; the remaining four
are additional poses. These are repeated configurations, not 40 people.
Source attribution: Stegmann MB and Gomez DD (2002), A Brief Introduction
to Statistical Shape Analysis, Informatics and Mathematical Modelling,
Technical University of Denmark (DTU).
https://github.com/kisungyou/Riemann/blob/e847692576f8b250c8140e1343b145f72caa379f/R/data_hands.R
Riemann's dataset help does not supply a separate public-domain dedication
for hands. The penguin or MNE CC0 notices must not be applied to it.

ERP
---
216 covariance matrices of size 32 by 32, with labels LA, LV, RA, RV
(57, 57, 53, 49 epochs respectively). The underlying MNE sample audiovisual
recording is from ONE participant. A reconstruction documented in Riemann's
September-21-2026 provenance update identifies supervised xDAWN filtering
and prototype augmentation of MEG data, not 32 original EEG sensors.
The matrices comprise 16 prototype and 16 filtered-trial features.
The separate 72-epoch training set was used to learn the representation;
the packaged 216 test epochs are not independent participants.
Data values match the pinned release; the older release's EEG/channel/
participant description is superseded by this provenance reconstruction.

Historical recipe by Pedro Rodrigues, pyRiemann 0.2.7:
https://github.com/pyRiemann/pyRiemann/blob/176e766f540bd4c7846f38573165fc3d27fc69ca/examples/ERP/plot_embedding_EEG.py
The recipe explicitly uses meg=True, eeg=False, four xDAWN filters per
class, centered empirical covariance, and a 25% training split with seed 42.
The recipe code is BSD-3-Clause; no recipe code is redistributed here.
